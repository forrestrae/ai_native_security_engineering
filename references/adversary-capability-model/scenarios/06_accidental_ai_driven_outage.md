## Accidental AI‑Driven Outage

A non‑malicious but harmful scenario emerges when an AI system behaves unpredictably in a production environment. Examples include automated remediation loops scaling out of control, generative agents issuing destructive configuration changes, or autonomous optimization systems misinterpreting telemetry and cascading failures across distributed systems. Although not an adversary in the traditional sense, an accidental AI‑driven outage can resemble an attack in its speed, scope, and difficulty to contain. Misaligned objectives or insufficient guardrails may lead the system to trigger irreversible changes before operators can intervene.

### Overall Assessment

Accidental AI‑driven outages highlight the risks of autonomous systems operating at cloud scale without adequate oversight. While lacking malicious intent, such incidents can cause Peak‑level systemic impact, speed, and propagation characteristics, underscoring the need for robust governance, testing, and containment controls.
