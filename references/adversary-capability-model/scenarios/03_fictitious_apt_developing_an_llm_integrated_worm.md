## Fictitious APT Developing an LLM‑Integrated Worm

The third scenario considers a theoretical APT claiming to have developed a self‑propagating worm embedding its own lightweight language model. This worm conducts reconnaissance, selects targets, exploits vulnerabilities, and adapts its behavior locally without continuous command-and-control. Once released, such a system could propagate across the internet and cloud environments, reaching assets at speeds unattainable for human-directed campaigns.

In practice, this adversary would reach Peak levels across autonomy, scale, speed, systemic impact, durability, and economic efficiency. While its precision would remain less refined than human‑curated targeting, its broad reach and capacity for local decision‑making would create substantial ecosystem‑level instability.
