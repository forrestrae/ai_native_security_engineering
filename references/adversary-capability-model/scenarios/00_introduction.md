# Applied Scenarios for the AI-Enabled Adversary Capability Model

The following scenarios illustrate how the AECM‑HC model can be applied to real and hypothetical adversaries. They are designed to help readers understand how AI integration alters an adversary’s capability profile and how different threat actors may evolve as AI-enabled operations proliferate. These examples highlight the distinctions between traditional human-directed campaigns, AI‑augmented groups transitioning into machine‑speed operations, and speculative adversaries operating near the theoretical limits of autonomy and scale. Each scenario includes a narrative description, a dimension table reflecting assessed capability levels, and a brief review of the systemic risks posed by the adversary in its current and future forms.
