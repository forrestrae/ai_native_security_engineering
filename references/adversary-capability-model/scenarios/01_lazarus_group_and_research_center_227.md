## Lazarus Group and Research Center 227

Reports describe North Korea’s Research Center 227 as an initiative focused on cultivating AI‑enabled offensive tools and enhancing the operational tempo of its cyber units. Lazarus demonstrates the early stages of this transition. Their teams appear to be experimenting with AI-assisted reconnaissance, automated exploit generation, and improved tooling coordination. As Research Center 227 matures, continued integration of AI research may enable Lazarus to shift from episodic human‑directed campaigns to more adaptive, semi‑autonomous operations with faster learning cycles, allowing North Korea to orchestrate broader campaigns, react dynamically to defender responses, and scale operations across multiple sectors with reduced human overhead.

Lazarus reflects a blend of established nation‑state tradecraft and AI augmentation. The group sits near Peak levels on dimensions such as precision and systemic impact, while its automation and learning rate remain in the Advancing category. The expected trajectory suggests a migration of several dimensions toward Peak as Research Center 227 matures, particularly speed, scale, persistence, and the breadth of AI use across the kill chain.
