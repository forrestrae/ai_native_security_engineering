## Insider Threat Using Generative AI

An insider equipped with generative AI tools represents a distinct adversarial profile in which legitimate access combines with AI‑accelerated capability. Such an insider can use AI to draft highly persuasive internal communications, generate convincing falsified artifacts, automate reconnaissance against internal systems, and craft tailored scripts that escalate privileges or exfiltrate data. Because insiders begin with trusted access and environmental familiarity, AI strengthens their ability to avoid detection and exploit gaps in approval workflows. As generative models grow more capable of mimicking organizational language patterns and technical styles, an insider’s operational flexibility expands further. The threat increases further if the insider leverages AI to automate lateral movement or conceal traces within complex logging systems.

### Overall Assessment

An AI‑enabled insider is a high‑risk adversary whose impact is amplified by preexisting trust and contextual awareness. Although their scale is limited compared with external APTs, their precision, stealth, and persistence often approach Peak levels, making them difficult for traditional monitoring systems to detect.
