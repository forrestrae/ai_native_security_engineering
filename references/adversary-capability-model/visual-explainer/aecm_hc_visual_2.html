AI-Enabled Adversary Capability Model for Hyperscale Cloud

AECM-HC

A 12-dimension scoring framework for characterizing how AI augments adversary behavior in hyperscale cloud environments — across technical enablement, operational tempo, and impact potential. Complements MITRE ATT&CK with an adversary-perspective lens on AI-driven threats.

Dimension Taxonomy

Three capability groups, four dimensions each. Groups build on one another: technical enablement shapes operational execution, which drives impact.

Technical Enablement
T1
Sophistication
Complexity and novelty of AI-driven attack paths; from phishing automation to environment-aware, adaptive exploit chains.
T2
Automation / Autonomy
Degree of human-out-of-the-loop execution; from episodic AI assistance to fully autonomous end-to-end operations.
T3
Adaptivity / Learning Rate
Speed of tactical iteration when encountering resistance; AI-augmented campaigns can adjust in real time vs. human-paced cycles.
T4
Precision / Targeting Quality
Effectiveness of identifying high-value targets; AI infers relationships between identities, services, and data flows at scale.
Operational
O1
Scale
Breadth of concurrent activity; automated campaigns can probe thousands of targets simultaneously across tenants and regions.
O2
Speed / Tempo
Rate of progression through the intrusion lifecycle; high tempo compresses defender dwell time and response windows.
O3
Stealth / Detectability
Ability to minimize observable footprint; AI shapes traffic patterns and behavioral signatures to blend with legitimate activity.
O4
Kill-Chain Coverage
Pervasiveness of AI across all intrusion phases — from reconnaissance and delivery through command-and-control and objectives.
Impact
I1
Blast Radius / Systemic Impact
Horizontal spread across tenants and vertical penetration toward privileged control surfaces or platform-level compromise.
I2
Persistence / Durability
Resilience of access when defenders rotate credentials or update infrastructure; AI manages redundancy and evasion.
I3
Economic Efficiency
Cost asymmetry between attacker operations and defender mitigation; Peak means near-zero marginal cost for extensive operations.
I4
Human / Social Exploitation
AI-enhanced impersonation, communication, and manipulation tightly integrated with technical intrusion channels.

Scoring Rubric

Three qualitative levels avoid false precision while maintaining consistency across evaluators. Levels represent meaningful shifts in operational maturity, not incremental gradations.

Foundational
Baseline conditions — AI has limited influence on attacker behavior. Operations are largely manual or rely on basic scripting. Conventional tradecraft with minimal AI augmentation.
Advancing
AI meaningfully shapes tactics and decision-making without dominating the intrusion lifecycle. Semi-autonomous workflows, context-aware actions, and measurable capability uplift.
Peak
Upper bound — automation, adaptivity, and operational reach converge. Highly sophisticated and efficient behavior with minimal human oversight requirements across the full intrusion lifecycle.
